Fiduciary Accountability

Our Standards of Financial Stewardship

We hold ourselves to rigorous standards of transparency, volunteer leadership, and ethical allocation.

⚖️

$0 Director Remuneration

Pursuant to Section 8 of our Articles of Incorporation, all Directors and Executive Officers serve on a 100% voluntary, non-remunerated basis ($0 fees).

🏛️

Federal NFP Compliance

Incorporated under the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act (Corp # 1154010-6, BN 779321272RC0001) in good standing with Corporations Canada.

🌍

Milestone Accountability

All international solidarity funds transferred to Burundi are governed by project agreements and verified vendor receipts before subsequent release.

📑

Transparent Reporting

Annual regulatory disclosures, corporate profiles, and financial transparency statements are publicly downloadable in both official languages.

Notice Regarding Non-Profit Status & Tax Receipts: Youth Building in Synergy to end Poverty (YBSP Canada) is a federally incorporated non-profit corporation. We are actively advancing our charitable registration application with the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA); direct donations do not currently generate charitable tax deduction receipts. We value complete honesty with our supporters.
