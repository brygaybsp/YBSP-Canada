Where Your Donation Goes

At YBSP Canada, financial integrity is a core value. In accordance with our non-profit governance mandate, all funds received are deployed to maximize community impact.

Youth Leadership & Field Programs 85%
Community Outreach & Events 10%
Fiduciary & Governance Compliance 5%
100% Voluntary Board Oversight

Per Section 8 of our federal Articles of Incorporation, our directors serve without remuneration. No director derives direct or indirect profits from their position, ensuring donor dollars stay on the frontlines.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Are donations tax-deductible in Canada? +

YBSP Canada is incorporated as a non-profit organization under the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act (Corporation # 1154010-6). For tax-receipted giving, donations can be facilitated through our registered philanthropic partners and platforms like CanadaHelps. We issue official donation acknowledgments for all gifts received directly.

How does YBSP transfer funds to Burundi initiatives? +

All international project transfers are governed by structured bilateral agreements with our sister organization, YBSP Burundi. Funds are released strictly against verified milestones (school fee receipts, vocational toolkit delivery logs, and participant verification) with photographic proof and independent financial tracking.

Can I set up recurring monthly donations? +

Yes. Monthly giving provides consistent, dependable resources that allow us to run youth cohorts year-round. You can toggle "Monthly Support" on our donation widget or set up automated monthly Interac transfers with your financial institution.

Does YBSP accept corporate matching or sponsorships? +

Yes. Many Canadian employers match charitable contributions made by their employees. Contact our partnership team at info@ybspcanada.org to coordinate corporate matching, grant sponsorships, or employee giving campaigns.
