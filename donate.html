Notice Regarding Legal Non-Profit Status & Tax Receipts

Youth Building in Synergy to end Poverty (YBSP Canada) is a federally incorporated not-for-profit corporation under the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act (NFP Act). We are not yet a registered charity with the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA); direct contributions currently do not generate official tax deduction receipts unless facilitated through our registered charitable partner funds. We prioritize complete transparency with our donors.

Fiduciary Accountability

Our Stewardship Principles

As a non-profit corporation governed by Section 8 of our Articles of Incorporation, our leadership and board serve without remuneration. Contributions are directed into verifiable youth empowerment and community development.

How are international funds to Burundi accounted for?

All international transfers to YBSP Burundi are governed by formal milestone agreements. Disbursements are released directly against vendor invoices and verified participant delivery logs, ensuring complete fiduciary auditability.

Can businesses sponsor a program cohort?

Yes. Canadian corporations, financial institutions, and employers can sponsor specific 12-week youth cohorts or workshop modules. Please visit our Institutional Partnerships section.

Where can I inspect public corporate filings?

YBSP Canada's federal regulatory records, including our corporation number (1154010-6) and annual returns, can be reviewed on our Corporate Filings page.
