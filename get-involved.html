Democratic Governance

Voting Membership Application

Under the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act, YBSP Canada is owned collectively by its registered members. Membership confers voting rights at Annual General Meetings, fiduciary oversight, and eligibility to serve on working committees and the Board of Directors.

Membership Criteria & Governance Rights

Membership is open to individuals aged 18+ residing in Canada who endorse our charter mandate of youth empowerment and poverty alleviation.

  • Voting Rights: One vote per registered member on corporate resolutions and director elections.
  • AGM Participation: Access to annual audited financial reports and direct dialogue with the Board.
  • Review Process: Submitting this application does not confer instantaneous membership. Pursuant to corporate by-laws, all applications are formally reviewed and approved by the Board of Directors.
