Our Mission & Core Values

Rooted directly in our federal Letters Patent and Articles of Incorporation under the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act.

Official Mission Statement

Youth Empowerment. Community Synergy. Poverty Alleviation.

"Youth Building in Synergy to end Poverty (YBSP Canada) mobilizes young people as active drivers of social and economic change. Grounded in our federal charter, our mission is to empower youth through mentorship, social entrepreneurship, and awareness on mental health and environmental stewardship, while fostering direct international solidarity to improve access to education, basic necessities, and sustainable livelihoods for vulnerable children and women in Canada and Burundi."

Bilateral Heritage

International Sisterhood with YBSP Burundi

YBSP’s roots began in Burundi in 2011. While YBSP Canada is an autonomous federal non-profit corporation, both entities share a common heritage, mission, and bilateral commitment to youth empowerment.

Through milestone-verified agreements, Canadian solidarity supports on-the-ground programs led by the local Burundian team: vocational starter equipment (#MurabireKazoza), school kit sponsorships for vulnerable children (#SororaIkaye), and women's economic solidarity cooperatives.
