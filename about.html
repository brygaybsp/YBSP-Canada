A federally incorporated non-profit organization dedicated to youth empowerment, community solidarity, and eradicating poverty through collective synergy in Canada and Burundi.

Our Foundation

Statutory Purpose & Core Mandate

Official Declaration of Intent (Letters Patent / Articles of Incorporation)

"Participer dans les campagnes de sensibilisation de la jeunesse sur différents maux (la pauvreté, la santé mentale, la pollution de l'environnement, etc.) qui minent notre société et comment les prévenir."

"Organiser des événements, des petits commerces, des levées de fonds pour soutenir les enfants et femmes défavorisés au Canada et dans d'autres pays du tiers monde pour qu'ils puissent avoir accès à l'éducation, le logement, l’électricité, l'eau potable, la nourriture, etc."

Source: Industry Canada / Corporations Canada — Articles of Incorporation (Form 4001, NFP Act).

Our Vision for Canadian Youth

We envision an inclusive society where youth are not passive recipients of aid, but active builders of economic self-sufficiency. By equipping youth—particularly diaspora members, newcomers, and equity-deserving groups—with practical mentorship, vocational skills, and leadership platforms, YBSP transforms vulnerability into community resilience.

In our Ottawa-Gatineau home region and across Canada, our cohorts learn to lead community dialogues, champion mental well-being, promote environmental stewardship, and launch sustainable community ventures.

Guiding Principles

The Pillars of Our Synergy

01

Collective Synergy

Rooted in the philosophy that lasting poverty alleviation requires uniting grassroots actors, diaspora knowledge, and local Canadian institutions.

02

Youth Leadership

Placing young people at the helm of design and decision-making for every project, workshop, and solidarity initiative.

03

International Solidarity

Directly connecting Canadian community action with transformative, on-the-ground youth empowerment in Burundi.

04

Fiduciary Integrity

Uncompromising transparency, voluntary board governance, and compliance under the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act.

Cross-Border Impact

Our Sisterhood with YBSP Burundi

YBSP was originally born from grassroots youth movements tackling extreme poverty in Burundi. YBSP Canada serves as an essential bridge—channeling diaspora resources, technology, skills exchange, and philanthropic partnerships to strengthen youth-led community projects across Burundi.

  • Direct funding for youth vocational workshops and micro-enterprise toolkits.
  • Educational access programs supporting vulnerable children and women.
  • Bilateral youth mentorship dialogues between Canadian and Burundian youth leaders.
