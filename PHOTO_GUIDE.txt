YBSP CANADA — COMPREHENSIVE WEBSITE PHOTO REPLACEMENT GUIDE (V32)
====================================================================

Every photo on the website is configured to look inside the `images/`
folder first. If you drop your real photo into the folder using the
exact file names listed below, the website will AUTOMATICALLY display
your real photo instead of the placeholder!

If any photo is not provided yet, the website automatically displays
a high-resolution fallback placeholder — so the site never looks broken!

- photo2.jpg -> The 2nd photo in the Lightbox
- photo3.jpg -> The 3rd photo in the Lightbox

HOW TO PUBLISH YOUR REAL PHOTOS:
1. Copy your JPG photos into the respective folders matching the filenames above.
2. Open `events.html` or `programs.html` in your browser to verify your photos appear.
3. Commit and push the `images/` folder to GitHub to deploy to Netlify!
