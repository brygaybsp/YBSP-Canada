================================================================================
                    YBSP CANADA — MASTER WEBSITE PHOTOGRAPHY GUIDE
================================================================================

Welcome! This guide explains the exact folder structure and image filenames used across 
the YBSP Canada website (https://ybspcanada.org/). 

All major photography is now organized into dedicated category folders under `images/`. 
To replace any image, simply drop your photograph into the corresponding folder using 
the exact filename listed below. 

All image references are equipped with automatic fail-safe fallbacks: if a local file 
is ever absent, the website seamlessly displays high-resolution community photography 
so no page will ever break or show a missing icon.

SUMMARY OF QUICK REPLACEMENTS:
- To replace Pillar photos: drop files into `images/programs/`
- To replace Event photos: drop files into `images/events/`
- To replace Album photos: drop files into `images/albums/<album-name>/`
- To replace News photos: drop files into `images/news/`
- To replace Hero/Bridge: drop files into `images/hero/` and `images/bridge/`
- To replace Leadership: drop files into `images/` or `images/team/`
