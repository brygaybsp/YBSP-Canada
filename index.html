Our Roots & Identity

One Movement. Two Continents. Shared Determination.

Founded in Burundi in 2011 as a youth-led humanitarian initiative and federally incorporated in Canada in 2019, YBSP bridges grassroots East African solidarity with practical community opportunity in Canada.

Canada • Ottawa-Gatineau

Community Support & Youth Opportunity

Building practical pilot programs in the Ottawa-Gatineau region focused on youth mentorship, digital skills, food relief, and pathways toward greater self-sufficiency.

The Human Heart of Our Mission
“Behind every initiative is a person working toward a more secure future. When we eliminate barriers to education, meals, and practical skills, human potential turns into lasting opportunity.”

Whether connecting youth in Gatineau with career mentors or distributing vocational toolkits to young artisans in Bujumbura, YBSP believes in the agency, dignity, and leadership of the people we walk alongside.

Documented Field Initiatives

Action Rooted in Tangible Community Needs

YBSP mobilizes community volunteers and pooled solidarity to deliver practical, verifiable solutions on the ground.

Bujumbura • Humanitarian Action

Sunday Meal Program

The Need:

Vulnerable youth and street children facing acute weekend food insecurity and isolation.

What YBSP Did:

Mobilized volunteer youth teams to pool resources, prepare nutritious meals, and provide dignified fellowship.

The Impact:

Over a decade of consistent community meals, fostering belonging and immediate nutrition.

Field History & Archives →
Education Access • Burundi

#SororaIkaye School Supply Drive

The Need:

Children from economically disadvantaged households prevented from schooling by notebook and supply shortages.

What YBSP Did:

Organized public solidarity campaigns to collect, assemble, and distribute complete school kits directly to students.

The Impact:

Enabled hundreds of young learners to enter classrooms equipped with pride and learning materials.

Education Initiatives →
Vocational Independence • East Africa

#MurabireKazoza Artisan Toolkits

The Need:

Young vocational graduates in carpentry, tailoring, and metalwork unable to start work due to prohibitive equipment costs.

What YBSP Did:

Equipped graduates with essential starter toolkits, sewing machines, and carpentry tools upon completion of trade training.

The Impact:

Transitioned young artisans from unemployment into self-sufficient, productive tradespeople.

Our Evolution

15 Years of Grassroots Persistence

How a youth-led community initiative in East Africa evolved into an active, federally incorporated Canadian non-profit.

2011
Founded in Burundi

Youth in Bujumbura unite voluntarily to respond to extreme poverty, street homelessness, and school dropouts.

2017
Diaspora Bridge Formed

Founder relocates to Canada, establishing personal and community networks while YBSP Burundi continues local action.

2019
Federal Incorporation

YBSP Canada officially incorporated under the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act (Corporation # 1154010-6).

Today
Canadian Program Pilots

Building volunteer-led community initiatives and partnerships in Ottawa-Gatineau while sustaining bilateral solidarity.

Organizational Stage & Transparency:

YBSP Canada is an autonomous, volunteer-led federal non-profit organization building its first Canadian program pilots, community partnerships, and foundation for future charitable registration. Some initiatives described are active pilots, while others are in planning or dependent on future community funding.
