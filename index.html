Building the Canadian Chapter of YBSP

Youth Building in Synergy to end Poverty (YBSP Canada) brings together youth, diaspora leaders, and community partners across Canada. We believe young people are the ultimate catalysts for economic self-sufficiency. Through practical mentorship, social entrepreneurship training, and direct international solidarity, we work to break the cycle of poverty at home and abroad.

Our Purpose

Why YBSP in Canada?

Canada is home to a growing, diverse generation of young people, newcomers, and members of the African diaspora. While Canada offers tremendous opportunity, many youth encounter real barriers in accessing executive career networks, mentorship, and commercial pathways.

YBSP Canada creates practical spaces where youth connect, build transferable skills, and launch community initiatives—while cultivating an authentic, direct bond of solidarity with youth-led grassroots projects in Burundi.

"We connect local Canadian youth development with meaningful global impact—giving youth the agency to transform their local communities and support international peers."

Fiduciary Ethics

Governance & Legal Status in Canada

Youth Building in Synergy to end Poverty is a federally incorporated not-for-profit corporation under the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act (NFP Act).

Our Board of Directors serves on a strictly voluntary, non-remunerated basis as set out in our Articles of Incorporation. We are committed to transparency, open communication, and annual statutory compliance.

Notice regarding tax receipts: YBSP Canada is currently incorporated as a non-profit corporation under federal law. We are not yet registered as a charitable entity with the CRA; direct gifts currently do not produce official tax deduction receipts unless facilitated through our registered philanthropic partners.
