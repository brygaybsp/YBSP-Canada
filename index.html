Our Evolution

A Journey of Purpose and Persistence

How a youth-led community initiative in East Africa evolved into an active, federally incorporated Canadian non-profit.

2011
Founded in Burundi

Youth in Bujumbura unite voluntarily to respond to extreme poverty, street homelessness, and school dropouts.

2017
Diaspora Bridge Formed

Founder relocates to Canada, establishing personal and community networks while YBSP Burundi continues local action.

2019
Federal Incorporation

YBSP Canada officially incorporated under the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act (Corporation # 1154010-6).

Today
Building in Canada

Building volunteer-led community initiatives and partnerships in Ottawa-Gatineau while sustaining bilateral solidarity.

Organizational Stage & Transparency:

YBSP Canada is an autonomous, volunteer-led federal non-profit organization building its first Canadian program pilots, community partnerships, and foundation for future charitable registration. Some initiatives described are active pilots, while others are in planning or dependent on future community funding.
