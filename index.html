About YBSP Canada

Building Sustainable Pathways Out of Poverty Through Youth Leadership

Incorporated under the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act (Corporation # 1154010-6), Youth Building in Synergy to end Poverty (YBSP) mobilizes youth as primary changemakers against systemic economic insecurity.

Working in direct solidarity with our sister organization YBSP Burundi, we combine Canadian youth leadership with international grassroots experience, delivering high-impact mentorship, professional skills, and community solidarity.

✓
Synergy & Inclusion Connecting diaspora, newcomers, and Canadian youth.
✓
Economic Skills Workforce readiness, digital literacy, and business tools.
✓
International Solidarity Empowering vulnerable youth communities in Burundi.
✓
Rigorous Governance Accountable, transparent board oversight and reporting.
