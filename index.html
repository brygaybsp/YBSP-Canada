Who We Are

Rooted in Community, Focused on Practical Opportunity

Founded in Burundi in 2011, YBSP was created to help address poverty and disadvantage. Today, YBSP Canada is building a Canadian organization focused on practical community support, education and pathways to opportunity, while maintaining a meaningful connection to YBSP's history and international work.

Our Purpose

Our Public Mission

"YBSP Canada works to relieve poverty and reduce barriers to opportunity by providing education, community support, mentorship, skills development and pathways toward employment, entrepreneurship and greater self-sufficiency."

We focus particularly on youth and individuals facing social and economic disadvantage, while working with community partners to create practical opportunities for people to learn, participate, contribute and build more secure futures.

Organizational Stage & Transparency:

YBSP Canada is a volunteer-led organization building its first Canadian program pilots, partnerships, and charitable-registration foundation. Some initiatives described on this website are active, while others are under development or dependent on future funding.

Community Focus

Who We Serve

YBSP Canada creates practical pathways for people facing systemic social and economic obstacles to participation and advancement.

Youth Facing Barriers to Opportunity

Young people navigating career transitions, lacking professional networks, or needing guidance to unlock their potential.

Individuals & Families in Disadvantage

Community members working through economic hardship who benefit from essential support and community connection.

People Facing Economic Barriers

Individuals seeking to build market-relevant workplace capabilities, financial literacy, and sustainable employment skills.

Community Members in Hardship

Individuals and households experiencing food insecurity or acute isolation who need dignified, welcoming community assistance.

Aspiring Learners & Entrepreneurs

Motivated individuals seeking practical education, mentorship, and small-enterprise pathways toward long-term self-sufficiency.

Inclusive Community Support

While newcomers and diaspora communities may benefit from specific programs, our doors are open to all facing disadvantage.
