Workforce & Leadership

Youth Mentorship & Career Readiness Cohorts

Bridging the transition from education into meaningful economic participation for newcomer, diaspora, and equity-deserving youth.

Operating across Gatineau, Ottawa, and virtual cohorts, this 12-week program connects participants with seasoned public administration and private sector professionals. Youth receive 1-on-1 resume optimization, interview coaching, networking strategies, and essential digital business tools.

✓

1-on-1 Executive Mentorship: Weekly pairing with established Canadian professionals.

✓

Workforce Soft Skills: Negotiation, public speaking, Canadian workplace culture, and networking.

✓

Applied AI & Digital Competencies: Workplace generative AI tools, prompt engineering for research, workflow automation, and ethical AI productivity.

Economic Agency

Social Entrepreneurship & Micro-Venture Lab

Fostering small business innovation and community-oriented commerce directly tied to our statutory mandate.

As articulated in our federal Letters Patent, YBSP organizes small enterprise initiatives and community fundraising to build financial autonomy. Our incubator guides youth innovators from ideation to launch, focusing on ventures that address local social needs while generating sustainable revenues.

✓

AI for Lean Startups & Micro-Grants: Leveraging low-cost AI tools for market research, branding, and business modeling, backed by micro-grant access.

✓

Financial Literacy & Compliance: Budgeting, bookkeeping, CRA tax basics, and Canadian business structures.

✓

Community Pop-Ups: Showcasing youth-crafted goods and services at regional community markets.

Public Education & Advocacy

Youth Awareness: Poverty, Mental Health & Sustainability

Direct fulfillment of our charter mandate to educate young Canadians on the root causes of systemic poverty and methods for prevention.

Poverty is multifaceted. Our youth awareness campaigns tackle the intersection of economic marginalization, youth mental health challenges, and urban environmental degradation. Through community dialogues, podcasts, and digital campaigns, youth become vocal advocates for holistic community well-being.

✓

Mental Health Resilience Forums: Destigmatizing mental health in racialized and newcomer families.

✓

Environmental Clean-Up & Ecology: Youth-led shoreline cleanups in Gatineau-Ottawa and urban gardening initiatives.

✓

Youth Policy Briefs: Publishing community findings to inform regional social development boards.

International Development

Global Synergy Initiative: Empowering Burundi Communities

Extending Canadian generosity and diaspora synergy to support vulnerable children and women in Burundi.

In direct collaboration with YBSP Burundi, our Canadian team channels targeted resources to grassroots initiatives. We support basic infrastructure, clean drinking water access, educational sponsorship for girls, and vocational tools for youth striving to break the cycle of extreme poverty.

✓

Education Access Sponsorship: Covering school fees, uniforms, and books for over 150 children in Bujumbura and rural provinces.

✓

Vocational Toolkits: Supplying carpentry, tailoring, and agricultural starter packages for young graduates.

✓

Women’s Economic Cooperatives: Supporting women-led collective savings and small trade associations.

Strategic Alignment

Aligned with Canadian & Global Impact Goals

YBSP Canada structures its programs to directly advance Canada's 2030 Agenda and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

SDG 1

No Poverty

Equipping youth with viable livelihoods and supporting extreme poverty alleviation in Burundi.

SDG 4

Quality Education

Digital literacy, applied AI competencies, and school sponsorships for vulnerable children.

SDG 8

Decent Work & Growth

Career mentorship, workplace soft skills, and community micro-enterprise incubation.

SDG 17

Partnerships for Goals

Bilateral Canada-Burundi solidarity and multi-sector alliances with public & private partners.
