Career Readiness & Digital Equity

Youth Mentorship, Career Readiness & Applied AI

Bridging the gap between education and quality employment for newcomer, diaspora, and equity-deserving youth across Canada.

Our structured 12-week mentorship cohort pairs participants with established Canadian public administration, technology, and business leaders. Participants gain practical skills in modern digital workflows, resume tailoring, mock technical interviews, and Canadian workplace culture.

  • ✓ 1-on-1 Executive Mentorship: Weekly coaching with Canadian professionals.
  • ✓ Applied AI Workplace Literacy: Practical generative AI tools, prompt engineering for research, workflow automation, and digital ethics.
  • ✓ Career Navigation: Tailoring academic backgrounds for federal, provincial, and private hiring channels.

Economic Self-Reliance

Social Entrepreneurship & Micro-Venture Lab

Fostering youth-led community commerce and small enterprise innovation in direct fulfillment of our federal charter.

Our incubator guides youth from ideation to launching micro-ventures that solve community needs. We combine financial literacy, compliance basics, and lean startup tools to help young entrepreneurs build sustainable local enterprises without heavy startup debt.

  • ✓ AI for Lean Startups: Leveraging free AI tools for low-cost market testing, business planning, and brand design.
  • ✓ Financial Literacy & CRA Compliance: Bookkeeping, sales tax fundamentals, and corporate structure basics.
  • ✓ Seed Validation Support: Mentorship guidance to validate prototypes and connect with regional micro-grant programs.

Charter Mandate

Community Health, Mental Wellness & Ecology

Direct implementation of our charter mandate to conduct educational campaigns addressing poverty, youth mental health, and environmental protection.

Poverty and economic marginalization are closely intertwined with health and environmental vulnerabilities. Through bilingual community roundtables and youth-led initiatives, we foster holistic community well-being and civic responsibility.

  • ✓ Mental Health Destigmatization: Culturally safe bilingual workshops addressing youth anxiety, burnout, and intergenerational dialogue.
  • ✓ Urban Environmental Action: Youth-led shoreline stewardship and watershed cleanups along Canadian waterways.
  • ✓ Youth Policy Dialogues: Fostering civic participation to inform community social planning boards.

International Solidarity

Global Synergy Initiative: Burundi Projects

Channeling Canadian solidarity and diaspora resources to fund verifiable grassroots youth projects in Burundi.

In direct collaboration with YBSP Burundi, our international synergy pillar targets tangible self-reliance. We support school kit sponsorships for vulnerable children and supply vocational starter equipment to young graduates in rural communities.

  • ✓ #SororaIkaye Education Program: Providing school supplies, uniforms, and fee support for children and girls in rural Burundi.
  • ✓ #MurabireKazoza Vocational Starter Kits: Outfitting young graduates with tailoring, carpentry, and agricultural toolkits.
  • ✓ Women's Solidarity Cooperatives: Community savings circles (tontines) enabling women to establish micro-trades.

Strategic Policy Alignment

Alignment with UN Sustainable Development Goals

YBSP Canada intentionally maps its operational outcomes to Canada's 2030 Agenda and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), ensuring grant readiness and international policy alignment.

SDG 1

No Poverty

Empowering youth with sustainable workforce skills and supporting grassroots micro-enterprise toolkits in Burundi.

SDG 4

Quality Education

Applied AI literacy, digital competencies in Canada, and #SororaIkaye school sponsorships in Burundi.

SDG 8

Decent Work & Growth

Career mentorship cohorts, Canadian workplace readiness, and community enterprise incubation.

SDG 17

Partnerships for Goals

Direct bilateral solidarity with YBSP Burundi and multi-sector alliances with Canadian institutions.
