Our Work & Core Pillars

Practical pathways to community support, education, skills, mentorship, employment, entrepreneurship and greater self-sufficiency across Canada.

Pillar 1: Immediate Relief & Essentials

Community Support & Poverty Relief

Practical initiatives that help address poverty, food insecurity, hardship and barriers to participation in community life. We believe that addressing basic community needs is an essential foundation for human dignity and long-term opportunity.

YBSP Canada works with community partners and volunteers to help address food insecurity and social isolation through community meals and other practical initiatives.

Key Program Focus Areas:

  • Community meals and practical food distribution initiatives
  • Partnerships with local food banks, shelters and frontline charities
  • Direct assistance and outreach for community members experiencing hardship
  • Volunteer-led community care and isolation reduction
🌱 Developing & Emerging Community Initiatives
Pillar 2: Learning & Mentorship

Education, Skills & Youth Opportunity

Education, mentorship and skills development that help people build confidence, knowledge, networks and pathways toward opportunity. This pillar is where our structured mentorship cohorts and community workshops live.

Participants explore practical career readiness, workplace communication, financial capability, and digital literacy. As part of modern digital skills, participants may also explore practical digital and AI tools that can support learning, work and productivity.

Key Program Focus Areas:

  • One-on-one youth mentorship pairing with experienced professionals
  • Career exploration, resume crafting, and interview readiness
  • Essential workplace skills, professionalism, and professional networking
  • Financial literacy, budgeting, and personal financial capability
  • Practical digital tools and AI literacy as a productivity resource
Join as Mentor or Mentee
Pillar 3: Economic Independence

Pathways to Self-Sufficiency

Employment readiness, entrepreneurship, financial capability and other pathways that can help people build greater economic independence. We work with individuals seeking to transition from systemic barriers into sustainable livelihoods.

Within this pillar, entrepreneurship education is presented as one valuable pathway toward economic self-sufficiency, alongside traditional employment preparation, career development, and small-business capability.

Key Program Focus Areas:

  • Employment preparation, job search strategies, and employer connections
  • Practical entrepreneurship education and micro-business fundamentals
  • Financial capability, bookkeeping basics, and sustainable cash management
  • Social enterprise development for community-benefiting ventures
  • Future micro-grant and seed support frameworks where funding permits

International Solidarity

Two Organizations. One Shared History. Complementary Work.

YBSP Canada and YBSP Burundi share a common history and commitment to reducing poverty and expanding opportunity. Each organization operates according to its own legal, governance and operational responsibilities.

🇨🇦

YBSP Canada Operations

YBSP Canada is an independent Canadian nonprofit organization focused on reducing poverty, expanding opportunity and strengthening communities in Canada, while supporting appropriate international initiatives.

Canadian donations fund Canadian community initiatives, with distinct international cooperation agreements governing cross-border solidarity projects.

🇧🇮

YBSP Burundi Field Programs

YBSP Burundi is the organization founded in Burundi in 2011, continuing locally rooted work supporting vulnerable children, families, youth and communities.

Global Framework Reference

UN Sustainable Development Goals Alignment

As a secondary reference framework, our domestic and bilateral activities align with key global sustainable development targets.

SDG 1 No Poverty Community support and relief for disadvantaged individuals.
SDG 4 Quality Education Skills development, mentorship, and practical digital learning.
SDG 8 Decent Work Employment readiness, self-sufficiency, and entrepreneurship.
SDG 10 Reduced Inequalities Removing social barriers to upward economic mobility.
SDG 17 Partnerships Collaborative community alliances and bilateral synergy.
