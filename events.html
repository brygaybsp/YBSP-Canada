Past Initiatives & Milestones

A glimpse into the activities and community projects led by YBSP Canada volunteers and partners over recent years.

Summer 2026 • Ottawa, ON

Youth Career Mentorship Graduation

Celebrated 65 newcomer and diaspora youth who completed our 12-week workplace readiness and resume building cohort with executive mentors.

Spring 2026 • Gatineau, QC

Youth Eco-Action & Shoreline Cleanup

Mobilized 80+ volunteers to clean riverbank corridors in Gatineau, promoting environmental stewardship and civic youth engagement.

Winter 2025 • Bujumbura Synergy

Vocational Toolkits & School Supplies

Directly financed and delivered educational packs for 150 primary school students and apprenticeship toolkits for youth trades in Burundi.
