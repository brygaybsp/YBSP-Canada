Signature Annual Event

YBSP Canada Annual Solidarity Gala

Each December, YBSP Canada gathers youth fellows, community partners, diaspora leaders, and civic allies for an evening dedicated to celebrating youth achievements and raising essential program funding.

  • 🏆 Youth Fellow Awards: Honoring exceptional graduates from our career readiness cohorts.
  • 📊 Annual Accountability Report: Direct public reporting on fund distribution and outcomes.
  • 🤝 Philanthropic Synergy: Raising critical matching funds for Canadian cohorts and Burundi toolkits.

Bujumbura, Burundi Weekly Sundays
Feed A Child Initiative

Sunday Meal Program

Documented in YBSP archives since 2013: weekly community meals organized every Sunday to provide warm nutrition, clothing, and psychosocial reintegration to children living on the streets in Bujumbura. Over 60 Sunday meals organized reaching more than 1,000 children.

View Weekly Sunday Dispatches on Facebook ↗
Bujumbura Rural & Kabezi Annual Campaign
Education Access

#SororaIkaye Back-to-School Drive

From the Kirundi phrase "Gusorora ikaye" (sharing a notebook instead of a drink): mobilizing citizen solidarity to equip vulnerable children with notebooks, uniforms, school bags, and minerval tuition assistance to return to school.

Explore #SororaIkaye Partner Reports ↗
