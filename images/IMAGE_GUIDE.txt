HOW IMAGE REPLACEMENT WORKS:
Every photo on the website is configured to look in this "images/" folder first.
Whenever you want to replace any stock picture with your own real photograph:
1. Save your real photo into this "images/" folder.
2. Rename your photo file to match the EXACT filename listed in the table below.
3. Commit the file to GitHub. Your real photo will automatically appear live!
