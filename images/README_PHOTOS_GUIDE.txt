YBSP CANADA — WEBSITE IMAGE REPLACEMENT DIRECTORY MAP
=====================================================

HOW TO REPLACE ANY PHOTO ON THE WEBSITE WITH YOUR OWN REAL PICTURES:
--------------------------------------------------------------------
You can easily replace any stock photo across the site with your own authentic pictures from Burundi or Canada.

Simply copy your photo into this `images/` folder and name it using the exact filenames listed below.
When you upload your files to GitHub, the website will automatically display your real photos!
