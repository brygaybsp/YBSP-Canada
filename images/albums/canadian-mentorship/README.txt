====================================================================
YBSP CANADA PHOTO ALBUM: Canadian Youth Mentorship Cohorts
====================================================================

Drop photos of 1-on-1 mentorship, career workshops, AI training, and graduation celebrations.

To display your real photos in this album, simply drop your photos
here using these exact filenames:

1. cover.jpg  -> The cover image displayed on the card in events.html
2. photo1.jpg -> First photo displayed when the album Lightbox opens
3. photo2.jpg -> Second photo in the Lightbox
4. photo3.jpg -> Third photo in the Lightbox
5. photo4.jpg -> Fourth photo in the Lightbox

NOTE: If any of these files are not present, the website will
automatically display high-resolution placeholder photos until you
replace them!
